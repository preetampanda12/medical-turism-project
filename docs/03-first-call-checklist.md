# Coordinator: Pehli Call ki Checklist

Target: form aane ke **2 ghante ke andar** call. Call ka time Google Sheet ke "First contact at" mein bharo.

## Call se pehle (2 min)
- [ ] Sheet mein case padho: condition, city, services needed
- [ ] WhatsApp label `📞 Contacted` lagao

## Call par (10–15 min)

**1. Parichay**
"Namaste, main {naam}, Irynt se. Aapne {patient} ji ke liye form bhara tha. Main aapka personal coordinator hoon, shuru se aakhir tak main hi aapke saath rahunga."

**2. Bimari samjho (salah mat do, sirf suno aur likho)**
- Kya takleef hai, kab se?
- Ab tak kis doctor ko dikhaya, unhone kya kaha?
- Koi diagnosis ya surgery batayi gayi hai?
- Patient abhi kaisa hai: chal-phir sakta hai, travel kar sakta hai?
- ⚠️ Emergency jaisa lage → "Abhi 112 call karein ya nazdeeki hospital jaayein."

**3. Practical baatein**
- Kis shehar mein ilaj pasand hai? Koi hospital pehle se dimaag mein hai?
- Budget kitna hai? Insurance ya Ayushman card hai?
- Kab tak shuru karna hai?
- Kitne log saath aayenge?
- Rehne, travel, assistant, dawai, kis cheez ki madad chahiye?

**4. Agla step batao**
"Aap reports WhatsApp par bhej dijiye. Main apne doctor advisor se dikhwa kar 2–3 achhe hospitals ko bhejunga. 24–72 ghante mein opinion aur kharche ka estimate aa jaata hai. Har update aapko WhatsApp par milega."

**5. Transparency (zaroor bolo)**
"Hamari service aapke liye free hai. Hospital hamein facilitation fee dete hain, lekin hum aapko hospital ka original estimate hi dikhayenge."

## Call ke baad (5 min)
- [ ] Sheet: Status → `Contacted`, "First contact at" bharo, Notes mein summary likho
- [ ] WhatsApp par `/reports` bhejo
- [ ] "Next action" column mein likho kya karna hai aur kab tak

## Kabhi mat karna ❌
- Dawai ya ilaj ki salah dena
- "100% theek ho jaayega" jaisa promise karna
- Patient ki permission ke bina reports kisi ko bhejna
- Hospital ke estimate mein kuch jodna ya chhupana
- Kisi ek hospital ke liye zabardasti push karna

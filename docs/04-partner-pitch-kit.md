# Partner Pitch Kit: Hospitals, Stays, Cabs aur Baaki

Partner se milne jaao ya call karo to **`partner.html` (Print one-pager)** ka PDF saath rakho ya WhatsApp par bhejo.

Har partner ko dashboard ke **Partners** page par turant add karo. Status aage badhao: `Prospect → Contacted → Meeting done → MoU signed`, aur "Last contacted" date bharte raho.

---

## A. Hospitals

### Kisse baat karni hai?
Bade private hospitals mein aksar inme se koi ek team hoti hai:
- **International Patient Services / International desk**
- **Marketing / Business development** (domestic referrals)
- **Patient relations / Corporate tie-ups**

Hospital ki website, LinkedIn ya reception se is team ka number lo. Doctors se seedhe commission ki baat **kabhi mat karo.**

### Pehla email (English)
**Subject:** Patient referral partnership – Irynt (care coordination)

> Dear [Name],
>
> I'm [Your name], founder of Irynt, a care coordination service that helps patients from smaller cities in India and from abroad plan their complete treatment journey: hospital selection, travel, stay near the hospital, a personal assistant and follow-up.
>
> We are building a small network of accredited hospitals for [cardiac / oncology / orthopaedic] cases from [regions]. For each case, we collect and organise the patient's reports, share them with your team (with consent) for an opinion and estimate, and present your estimate to the family exactly as given. Patients pay the hospital directly.
>
> I'd value 20 minutes to understand your referral process and how we could work together. A one-page overview is attached.
>
> Regards,
> [Name] · [Phone / WhatsApp] · [Website]

### Pehli call ka script (Hinglish)
1. **Parichay (30 sec):** "Namaste, main [naam], Irynt se. Hum chhote shehron aur videsh ke patients ko sahi hospital tak pahunchate hain aur unka travel, rehna aur madad sambhalte hain."
2. **Unka process poochho:** "Aap referral partners ke saath kaise kaam karte hain? Case ka opinion kaun deta hai, kitne din mein?"
3. **Value batao:** "Patient ki reports organised aati hain, family ke sawaal hum sambhalte hain, aur patient taiyaar hokar time par pahunchta hai."
4. **Terms poochho:** "Facilitation partners ke liye aapki standard policy kya hai? Kya written agreement hota hai?"
5. **Next step:** "Kya main ek pilot case bhej sakta hoon? Aur agreement ka draft share kar dun?"

### Common sawaal aur jawab

| Wo kahein | Aap kaho |
|---|---|
| "Aap kitne patients laoge?" | "Hum abhi shuru kar rahe hain. Volume ka vaada nahi karunga, lekin har case properly prepared hoga. Pehle 2-3 pilot cases se dekhiye." |
| "Hamara khud ka international desk hai." | "Bilkul. Hum aapke desk ke saath kaam karte hain, competition mein nahi. Travel, stay, family aur follow-up ka kaam hum lete hain jo hospital ke scope se bahar hai." |
| "Commission kitna chahiye?" | "Jo aapki standard facilitation policy hai, wahi. Bas written ho, aur patient ke estimate mein kuch na juda ho." |
| "Pehle kisi aur facilitator se problem hui thi." | "Isliye hum patient ko aapka original estimate dikhate hain aur hamari fee transparent hai. Agreement mein yeh likha bhi hoga." |

### Follow-up (3 din baad, WhatsApp)
> Namaste [Name] ji, Irynt se [aapka naam]. Pichhli baat ke liye dhanyavaad. Agreement ka draft aur hamara one-pager bhej raha hoon. Kya is hafte 15 minute ki call ho sakti hai?

## B. Stays (guest house, service apartment, dharamshala)

**Kya dekhna hai:** hospital se 1–2 km, saaf kamre, AC/non-AC options, kitchen ya khane ki suvidha, lift (bujurg patients ke liye), 24x7 entry, mahine ka rate.

**Pitch:**
> "Hum hospital aane wale patients aur unke parivaar ko aapke yahan bhejenge, aksar 1–4 hafte ke liye. Hamein patient ke liye ek fixed, saaf aur transparent rate chahiye. Monthly stay par discount? Har booking par [X]% referral ya fixed fee par baat kar sakte hain."

**Pehli booking se pehle:** khud jaakar kamra dekho, photo lo aur notes mein likho.

## C. Cab operators
- Per-trip aur per-day rate likhwa lo (station/airport pickup alag se)
- Driver ka naam, number aur gaadi number booking se pehle patient ko bhejo
- Wheelchair ki zaroorat ho to badi gaadi ka option rakho

## D. Care assistants / translators
- ID proof aur ek reference zaroor lo
- Bhasha, din ka rate aur raat ki duty ka rate likho
- Pehle 1-2 cases mein khud bhi saath jaao

## E. Pharmacy
- Hospital ke paas wali pharmacy jo stay tak delivery kare
- Patient ka bill seedha pharmacy ka ho. Aap beech mein paisa mat lo.

## Partner onboarding checklist
- [ ] Dashboard mein partner add kiya (type, city, contact, rate, unit)
- [ ] Rate aur terms likhit mein (WhatsApp message bhi chalega shuru mein)
- [ ] Hospital ke liye: facilitation agreement signed (`docs/05-facilitation-agreement-template.md`)
- [ ] Stay ke liye: khud visit karke photo li
- [ ] Partner ko samjhaya ki patient ka data confidential hai
- [ ] Status `MoU signed` kiya

## Ethics rules (inhe kabhi mat todna)
- Doctors ko referral ke badle paisa ya gift **nahi** (NMC rules ke against hai)
- Patient ko hamesha original estimate dikhao
- Jo partner patient ke saath bura bartav kare ya overcharge kare, use `Inactive` kar do

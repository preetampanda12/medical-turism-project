# Patient Facilitation Agreement (Template)

> ⚠️ **Yeh ek DRAFT template hai, legal advice nahi.** Sign karne se pehle kisi lawyer se zaroor review karwao. Bahut se hospitals ka apna format hota hai. Wo dein to unka padho aur neeche ke points se compare karo.

---

**PATIENT FACILITATION AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made on **[DATE]** between:

**[HOSPITAL LEGAL NAME]**, having its registered office at [ADDRESS] ("the Hospital"), and

**[IRYNT REGISTERED BUSINESS NAME]**, having its office at [ADDRESS] ("Irynt").

### 1. Purpose
Irynt provides care coordination and medical travel facilitation to patients who choose to seek treatment at the Hospital. Irynt does not provide medical services.

### 2. Irynt's responsibilities
2.1 Share patient information and medical records with the Hospital only with the patient's documented consent.
2.2 Present the Hospital's opinion and cost estimate to the patient **exactly as provided**, without mark-up.
2.3 Assist patients with travel, accommodation, local transport and documentation, at the patient's choice.
2.4 Not give medical advice, and not make any promise of medical outcome on behalf of the Hospital.
2.5 Keep patient information confidential and comply with applicable data protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

### 3. Hospital's responsibilities
3.1 Nominate a point of contact: [NAME, DESIGNATION, PHONE, EMAIL].
3.2 Provide a medical opinion and written cost estimate within [72] hours of receiving complete records, where possible.
3.3 Charge Irynt-referred patients the same as, or less than, comparable direct patients.
3.4 Inform Irynt of admission, discharge and final billing for referred patients, to the extent permitted by law and patient consent.
3.5 Issue medical visa invitation letters for eligible international patients.

### 4. Facilitation fee
4.1 The Hospital shall pay Irynt a facilitation fee of **[__% of the final hospital bill / fixed ₹____ per admitted patient]**, excluding [pharmacy / consumables / implants / taxes – specify].
4.2 Fees are payable within [30] days of the patient's discharge against Irynt's invoice.
4.3 The fee shall **not** be added to the patient's bill.
4.4 No fee or benefit shall be paid by either party to any individual doctor.
4.5 A patient is considered "referred by Irynt" if registered with Irynt's case ID within [__] days of Irynt first sharing their records.

### 5. Patient choice
Patients are free to choose any hospital, or none. Irynt will typically present more than one option.

### 6. Confidentiality and data
Both parties will keep patient data and the terms of this Agreement confidential, use patient data only for the patient's treatment and coordination, and delete or return it on request where the law allows.

### 7. Liability
7.1 The Hospital is solely responsible for medical services, clinical decisions and outcomes.
7.2 Irynt is responsible only for its own coordination services.
7.3 Each party shall indemnify the other against claims arising from its own negligence or breach. [LAWYER TO REVIEW]

### 8. Marketing
Neither party will use the other's name or logo without written approval. [Optional: The Hospital permits Irynt to name it as a partner hospital on its website.]

### 9. Term and termination
This Agreement is valid for [12] months and renews automatically unless either party gives [30] days' written notice. Fees for patients already referred remain payable.

### 10. Governing law
Laws of India. Courts at [CITY] shall have jurisdiction.

**Signed**

For the Hospital: ____________________ Name / Designation / Date

For Irynt: ____________________ Name / Designation / Date

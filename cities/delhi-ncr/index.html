Coming to Delhi NCR for treatment

Delhi, Gurugram, Noida and Faridabad have some of the biggest hospitals in the country. The hard part is the distances and the traffic, so we make sure your room is close to your hospital and you're not spending hours in a cab.

Getting to Delhi NCR

Indira Gandhi International Airport serves domestic and international flights. Major railway stations include New Delhi, Hazrat Nizamuddin, Old Delhi and Anand Vihar Terminal.

Just tell us when your train or flight gets in. Someone will be there to take you to your room or straight to the hospital.

A few things worth knowing

  • NCR is spread out. A hospital in Gurugram and a room in central Delhi can easily be an hour apart in traffic. Stay near the hospital, not near the station.
  • Summers (April–June) are very hot; winters (December–January) are cold and air quality can be poor. Patients with heart or lung conditions should plan accordingly.
  • Hindi is widely spoken; hospital staff commonly speak English too.

How to get an Indian medical visa

The e-Medical visa, step by step.

Written by the Irynt team · Updated 10 November 2026

Visa rules change and differ by nationality. Always check the latest rules on the Government of India's official visa website, indianvisaonline.gov.in, before applying. Beware of unofficial sites that charge extra fees.

Which visa do I need?

  • e-Medical visa: for the patient travelling for treatment. Under current rules it is generally issued for a limited period with multiple (triple) entries.
  • e-Medical Attendant visa: for family members accompanying the patient. Usually up to two attendants are allowed per patient, and attendants are expected to travel with the patient.
  • Nationals of some countries are not eligible for the e-Visa and apply for a regular medical visa through the Indian mission. Citizens of Nepal and Bhutan do not need a visa to enter India.

Documents you will usually need

  • Passport valid for at least six months, with blank pages
  • Recent passport-size photograph (as per the official specifications)
  • Letter from the Indian hospital on its letterhead, confirming the treatment
  • Medical reports from your home country
  • Attendants: passport, photo and relationship details

The hospital invitation letter

Once you choose a hospital, it issues a letter describing the patient's condition, the planned treatment and the expected duration. Irynt requests this letter from the hospital for you as part of your plan, and checks that names and passport details match exactly.

Step-by-step

  1. Share your reports and get hospital opinions and estimates.
  2. Choose a hospital and confirm the plan.
  3. Receive the hospital's letter.
  4. Apply online on the official website for the patient and attendants, and pay the fee.
  5. Wait for approval (the ETA) by email, then print it.
  6. Book flights; we arrange airport pickup and your stay.

After you arrive

Depending on the visa and the length of stay, registration with the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (done online through the e-FRRO system) may be required, and hospitals often help with this. If treatment takes longer than planned, extensions are handled through the same system with the hospital's support.

Practical tips

  • Apply well in advance, but within the window the official site allows before travel.
  • Make sure the passport name matches the hospital letter exactly.
  • Carry printed copies of the visa approval, hospital letter and reports.
  • Keep some local currency and arrange an Indian SIM card on arrival. We can help.

Common questions

How many attendants can travel with a patient on an e-Medical visa?

Under current rules, usually up to two attendants on e-Medical Attendant visas. Check the official website for the latest rules for your nationality.

Does Irynt apply for the visa for me?

No. You apply on the official government website. We arrange the hospital invitation letter and help you prepare the documents.

What if my treatment takes longer than my visa?

Extensions are possible in many cases through the e-FRRO system, with a supporting letter from the hospital. We help coordinate the letter.

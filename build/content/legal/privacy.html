1. What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone/WhatsApp number, email, city and country.
  • Patient details: the patient's name, age, gender and description of the health problem.
  • Medical records: reports, scans and prescriptions you choose to share with us.
  • Travel and stay details: travel dates, ID or passport details (only when needed for bookings or visa letters), and service preferences.
  • Insurance details: policy information, only if you ask for claim help.

2. Why we use it

  • To understand your needs and contact you.
  • To get medical opinions and cost estimates from hospitals you approve.
  • To arrange travel, stay, assistants, medicines, insurance paperwork and follow-up that you request.
  • To meet legal requirements.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your medical information for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Only with your consent, and only what is needed:

  • Hospitals and doctors you approve, for opinions and treatment.
  • Service partners you choose (stay, cab, pharmacy, insurance or loan partners), for those services only.
  • Government authorities, if the law requires it.

For patients outside India, your data may be processed in India, where our team and partner hospitals are located.

5. How we protect it

When is knee replacement considered?

Orthopaedic surgeons usually consider knee replacement when arthritis causes severe pain and stiffness that limit daily life, and when medicines, physiotherapy, weight management or injections no longer help enough.

Options include total knee replacement, partial (unicompartmental) replacement for selected patients, and replacing one or both knees in the same admission or in stages. The surgeon recommends the option after examining the patient.

Reports to share

  • Standing X-rays of both knees (front and side views)
  • Recent blood tests, blood sugar and ECG
  • List of current medicines and other conditions
  • Any earlier surgery on the knee

Recovery and how long to stay

Physiotherapy usually starts within a day or two of surgery, and most patients walk with support before discharge. Many surgeons review the wound and remove stitches or clips around two weeks after surgery, so families often plan about 2–3 weeks in the city.

A ground-floor room or a building with a lift, a western toilet and a short ride to the hospital make a real difference. We choose stays with these in mind.

What affects the cost

  • One knee or both knees
  • Type of implant chosen with the surgeon
  • Room category and days in hospital
  • Other health conditions that need extra care
  • Physiotherapy sessions after discharge

Common questions about knee replacement

Should both knees be replaced at the same time?

It depends on the patient's age, health and the surgeon's assessment. Some patients have both knees done in one admission; others in two stages. We share each hospital's recommendation and estimate for the options.

Can knee replacement be done under Ayushman Bharat or insurance?

Knee replacement is covered by many insurance policies and government schemes, at empanelled hospitals. We help check eligibility and handle pre-authorisation paperwork.

When can the patient climb stairs and travel?

Most patients climb stairs with support before discharge, but travel home depends on the surgeon's review. We plan the stay until that review.

Do you arrange physiotherapy after discharge?

Yes. We can arrange sessions at the hospital or at your stay, and help find a physiotherapist in your home city.

Do we have to pay Irynt?

No, our help is free. If you want a room, cabs or a helper, we'll tell you the price first. The hospital's estimate is passed on to you exactly as they gave it.

How cancer treatment is planned

Most large cancer centres in India plan treatment through a multidisciplinary team: surgical, medical and radiation oncologists review the reports together. The plan depends on the type of cancer, its stage, the patient's overall health and their preferences.

The main treatment options are:

  • Surgery to remove the tumour, sometimes with reconstruction.
  • Chemotherapy, usually given in cycles a few weeks apart.
  • Radiation therapy, often given on weekdays over several weeks.
  • Targeted therapy and immunotherapy for certain cancers, based on specific test results.

A second opinion before starting treatment is common and sensible. With complete reports, many hospitals can share an initial opinion before you travel.

Reports to share for an opinion

  • Biopsy / histopathology report, and immunohistochemistry (IHC) if done. This is the most important document.
  • Imaging: PET-CT, CT or MRI reports, and the scan images/CD if available.
  • Recent blood tests (complete blood count, kidney and liver function).
  • Treatment so far: discharge summaries, chemotherapy or radiation details, surgery notes.
  • A short note on current symptoms and other conditions (diabetes, heart disease).

Not sure which reports you have? Send whatever you have on WhatsApp. Your coordinator will tell you what is missing.

Planning a longer stay

The biggest practical challenge in cancer care is often the stay, not the hospital. A typical plan has three phases:

PhaseWhat happensWhat we arrange
EvaluationConsultation, scans and tests, team reviewShort stay near the hospital, cabs, appointment booking
TreatmentSurgery and/or chemo and radiation cyclesMonthly room rates, assistant on key days, medicine delivery, food options for the family
Follow-upScans and review visitsTravel planning for each visit, reminders, coordination with your local doctor

Whether some cycles can be continued closer to home is a medical decision. Ask the treating oncologist; we will plan around their answer.

What affects the cost

Cancer treatment costs vary more than almost any other treatment, so we don't publish a single price. The main factors are:

  • Type and stage of cancer, and the treatment plan chosen
  • Number of chemotherapy cycles and radiation sessions
  • Medicines used: generic or branded, targeted therapy or immunotherapy
  • Surgery type, length of hospital stay and room category
  • Scans and tests during treatment

We share each hospital's written estimate exactly as given and help you ask the right questions about what is included.

Cancer Treatment: frequently asked questions

Can I get a cancer treatment opinion before travelling?

Often, yes. If you share the biopsy report, scans and blood tests, many hospitals can give an initial opinion and estimate before you travel. A final plan is made after the doctor examines the patient.

How long will we need to stay for cancer treatment?

It depends on the plan. Evaluation may take several days; chemotherapy and radiation can continue for weeks or months. Your coordinator plans the stay phase by phase once the doctors share the treatment plan.

Can a family member stay with the patient?

Yes. We plan stays that work for both the patient and the person caring for them, including food options and help during busy hospital days.

Does insurance or Ayushman Bharat cover cancer treatment?

Many policies and government schemes cover cancer treatment, but coverage depends on the policy, the hospital and the procedure. We help with pre-authorisation and paperwork; the insurer decides what is covered.

Is Irynt's coordination free?

Yes. Coordination is free for patients. Optional services like stay, cabs or an assistant are priced upfront, and hospital estimates are shared exactly as given.

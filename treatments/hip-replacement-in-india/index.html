When is hip replacement considered?

Surgeons consider hip replacement for severe hip pain and stiffness from arthritis, avascular necrosis or certain fractures, when other treatment no longer helps. Revision surgery replaces an older implant that has worn out or loosened.

A fall followed by inability to stand or walk is an emergency. Please go to the nearest hospital first.

Reports to share

  • X-ray of the pelvis with both hips; MRI if done
  • Blood tests, blood sugar, ECG
  • Details of any earlier hip surgery or implant
  • Current medicines and other conditions

Recovery and how long to stay

Walking with support usually starts soon after surgery. Patients are taught movement precautions to protect the new joint. Plan for about 2–3 weeks in the city until the first review, in a stay with a lift or ground-floor room, a raised or western toilet and a short ride to the hospital.

What affects the cost

  • Primary replacement or revision surgery
  • Type of implant and bearing surface
  • Room category and days in hospital
  • Other health conditions
  • Physiotherapy after discharge

Hip Replacement: frequently asked questions

Is hip replacement different from knee replacement in recovery?

Both need physiotherapy and a few weeks of careful movement. Hip patients also learn specific precautions for sitting and bending. Your surgeon and physiotherapist will guide you.

Can elderly patients travel for hip replacement?

Many do. We plan travel with wheelchair assistance where available, a comfortable stay and help on admission and discharge days.

How do I compare hip replacement hospitals?

Look at the surgeon's experience with the specific surgery, accreditation, what the estimate includes and how follow-up will work. We put these side by side for you.

Is Irynt's coordination free?

Yes. Coordination is free for patients. Optional services like stay, cabs or an assistant are priced upfront, and hospital estimates are shared exactly as given.

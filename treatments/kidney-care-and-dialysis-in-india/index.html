Kidney care and dialysis in India

If you're on dialysis, every trip has to be planned around your sessions. If it's a kidney stone or a prostate problem, you need the right urologist. We can help with both.

What we help with

  • Dialysis while travelling: booking haemodialysis sessions near your stay, for patients travelling for any treatment
  • Kidney stones: opinions on treatment options such as lithotripsy or endoscopic procedures
  • Chronic kidney disease: nephrology opinions and long-term care planning
  • Prostate and urology conditions

Irynt does not arrange organ donors. Kidney transplantation in India follows a strict legal approval process under the Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act; we can only help approved families with travel and stay.

Dialysis on the move: what to send

  • Latest dialysis prescription (frequency, duration, dialyser, heparin)
  • Recent blood tests including viral markers (HBsAg, HCV, HIV), usually required by dialysis centres
  • Vascular access details (fistula or catheter)
  • Nephrologist's note and current medicines

Dialysis centres often have limited slots. Please tell us your travel dates as early as possible.

What affects the cost

  • Number of dialysis sessions and the centre chosen
  • For procedures: the technique, equipment and days in hospital
  • Tests and medicines

Common questions about kidney care & dialysis

Can you book dialysis for a patient travelling for another treatment?

Yes. Share the dialysis prescription and recent reports, and we book sessions at a centre close to the stay and the treating hospital.

Do you arrange kidney transplants?

We do not arrange donors. Transplants in India require legal approval from an authorisation committee. For approved cases, we can help with travel and stay only.

Which reports are needed for a kidney stone opinion?

Usually an ultrasound or CT scan report (KUB), urine and kidney function tests, and notes on symptoms.

Do we have to pay Irynt?

No, our help is free. If you want a room, cabs or a helper, we'll tell you the price first. The hospital's estimate is passed on to you exactly as they gave it.

Want us to look at your case?

Tell us what's going on. We'll call you back within 2 hours and take it from there.

Spine surgery in India

A lot of back and neck problems get better without surgery. Some don't. Either way, it's a decision that deserves a careful opinion, so we get your MRI seen by spine specialists before you travel.

Do I need spine surgery?

For many people, back and neck pain improves with time, medicines, physiotherapy or injections. Surgery is usually considered when there is severe nerve compression, weakness, or pain that does not improve with other treatment. Spine surgeons and neurosurgeons both treat spine conditions.

Sudden weakness in the legs, numbness around the groin, or loss of bladder or bowel control needs emergency care. Go to the nearest hospital immediately.

Common spine procedures

  • Discectomy / microdiscectomy for a slipped (herniated) disc
  • Decompression (laminectomy) for spinal stenosis
  • Spinal fusion for instability
  • Minimally invasive and endoscopic options for selected patients

Reports to share

  • MRI report and images of the affected area (neck or lower back)
  • X-rays, including any flexion-extension views
  • Notes on symptoms: pain, numbness, weakness, how long
  • Treatment so far: medicines, physiotherapy, injections

What affects the cost

  • Type of surgery and number of levels treated
  • Implants (screws, rods, cages) if fusion is needed
  • Minimally invasive or open technique
  • Days in hospital and room category

Common questions about spine surgery

Can I get a spine opinion online with my MRI?

Yes. Send your MRI report and images; we share them with spine specialists at 2–3 accredited hospitals for an opinion before you travel.

Is minimally invasive spine surgery always better?

Not always. It suits certain conditions and patients. The surgeon will recommend the technique that is safest and most effective for the specific problem.

How long should I plan to stay after spine surgery?

It ranges from a few days to two weeks or more, depending on the procedure. We plan the stay around the surgeon's advice.

Do we have to pay Irynt?

No, our help is free. If you want a room, cabs or a helper, we'll tell you the price first. The hospital's estimate is passed on to you exactly as they gave it.

Tell us what's going on. We'll call you back within 2 hours and take it from there.

Brain and neurosurgery in India

Hearing the word "brain" from a doctor is frightening. We help you reach experienced neuro specialists quickly, understand what they're suggesting, and plan the stay and rehab that often comes after.

Conditions we coordinate

  • Brain and pituitary tumours: surgery, radiation and follow-up
  • Epilepsy that does not respond to medicines: evaluation for surgery
  • Movement disorders such as Parkinson's disease: evaluation for procedures like deep brain stimulation in selected patients
  • Hydrocephalus, aneurysms and vascular conditions
  • Rehabilitation after stroke or brain surgery

Sudden weakness on one side, slurred speech, a severe sudden headache or a seizure needs emergency care now. Call 112.

Reports to share

  • MRI or CT brain report and images
  • Neurologist or neurosurgeon notes
  • EEG reports (for epilepsy)
  • Biopsy report, if any
  • Current medicines with doses

Planning the stay and rehabilitation

After brain surgery, patients may spend time in intensive care, then the ward, and may need physiotherapy, speech or occupational therapy. Families often need a longer stay than they expect. We plan accommodation close to the hospital, an assistant on the hardest days and transport suited to the patient's mobility.

What affects the cost

  • The condition and the procedure
  • Use of advanced technology (navigation, intraoperative imaging)
  • Days in ICU and ward
  • Radiation or other treatment after surgery
  • Rehabilitation needs

Common questions about brain & neurosurgery

Can I get a neurosurgery opinion with my MRI?

Yes. Share the MRI report and images and the doctor's notes. We send them to neuro specialists at 2–3 accredited hospitals for an opinion and estimate.

How long will the patient be in hospital after brain surgery?

It varies a lot by procedure and recovery. The neurosurgeon will give an estimate; we plan the family's stay accordingly and adjust as needed.

Do you help with rehabilitation after discharge?

Yes. We help arrange physiotherapy and other therapy sessions and plan follow-up visits.

Do we have to pay Irynt?

No, our help is free. If you want a room, cabs or a helper, we'll tell you the price first. The hospital's estimate is passed on to you exactly as they gave it.

Want us to look at your case?

Tell us what's going on. We'll call you back within 2 hours and take it from there.

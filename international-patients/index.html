Questions from international patients

Do you help with the Indian medical visa?

We arrange the hospital's invitation letter and help you prepare documents. You apply on the official Government of India visa website.

Can my family come with me?

Yes. Under current rules, usually up to two attendants can travel on e-Medical Attendant visas. We plan their stay too.

Do you provide translators?

Yes, on request. Tell us your language and we will arrange a translator for doctor visits.

How do I pay the hospital?

You pay the hospital directly as per its process. We explain the deposit and payment steps before you travel.

Get opinions before you travel

Tell us the problem. A personal coordinator calls you within 2 working hours with hospital options and a complete plan for travel, stay and support.

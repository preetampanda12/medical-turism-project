What happens, step by step

  1. Send your reports

    On WhatsApp or email. Our doctor advisor takes a look first.

  2. Hear from hospitals

    Two or three hospitals send their opinion and a written estimate.

  3. Get the visa letter

    Once you choose, we get the hospital's letter for your e-Medical visa.

  4. Land in India

    We meet you at the airport and help with a SIM card, money exchange and your room.

  5. Treatment, then home

    A translator if you need one, a daily call from us, and follow-up once you're back.

Questions from patients abroad

Can you get me the Indian medical visa?

We can't apply for you, but we get the hospital's invitation letter and help you get your papers in order. You then apply on the official Government of India visa website.

Can my family come with me?

Yes. Under the current rules, up to two family members can usually come on e-Medical Attendant visas. We'll find a place for them to stay too.

I don't speak English or Hindi. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Tell us your language and we'll arrange a translator for your doctor visits.

How do I pay the hospital?

You pay the hospital directly, the way they ask. Before you travel, we'll explain how much deposit they need and how the rest is paid.

Tell us what's going on. We'll call you back within 2 hours and take it from there.

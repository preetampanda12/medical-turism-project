We help families get the right treatment, without the chaos

Irynt is a care coordination service for patients travelling to another city or country for treatment. We exist because finding the right hospital should not depend on who you know.

Why we started Irynt

Every day, families from smaller towns travel to big cities for treatment. They arrive with a folder of reports, little information and a lot of fear. They spend days finding the right doctor, overpay for a room, and handle insurance paperwork alone, while also caring for someone who is unwell.

Irynt brings all of that into one plan, handled by one coordinator who stays with the family from the first call until follow-up at home.

What we promise

  • Original estimates. We share each hospital's estimate exactly as given.
  • Open about how we are paid. Hospitals and partners may pay us a facilitation fee; it is never added to your estimate.
  • No payments to doctors. We never pay or accept referral fees from individual doctors.
  • Doctors decide. We coordinate; qualified doctors make every medical decision.
  • Your data stays private. Reports go only to hospitals you approve.

How we choose partners

We work with hospitals accredited by recognised bodies such as NABH or JCI, and with stays, cab operators and assistants we have checked ourselves. Partners that treat patients poorly or overcharge are removed.

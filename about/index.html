About Irynt

We help families who have to travel for treatment. Finding the right hospital shouldn't depend on whether you happen to know a doctor in the big city.

Why we started

If you've ever taken a parent to a big city hospital, you'll know how it goes. You get off the train with a plastic folder full of reports and no idea where to start. The first few days go in finding the right doctor. The room near the hospital costs twice what it should. Someone has to stand at the insurance desk for hours. And all this while you're worried about the person you came with.

We started Irynt so families don't have to figure all of that out alone. One person from our team stays with you from the first phone call until you're back home, and handles the running around so you can focus on the patient.

What we promise you

  • You see the real estimate. Whatever the hospital quotes, you see it as it is.
  • We're open about money. Hospitals and some partners pay us a fee. It never gets added to your bill.
  • No deals with doctors. We don't pay doctors for referrals, and we don't take money from them either.
  • Doctors decide treatment. We'll never tell you which treatment to choose.
  • Your reports stay private. They only go to hospitals you've said yes to.

Who we work with

Only NABH or JCI accredited hospitals. For rooms, cabs and helpers, we visit and check them ourselves first. If a partner treats a patient badly or overcharges, we stop working with them.

Want us to help?

Send us your reports. We'll come back with two or three hospital options, what each will cost, and a plan for your stay.

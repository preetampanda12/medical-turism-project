How it works

From first call to coming home

Five simple steps. You can stop at any point, no questions asked.

  1. Tell us what's wrong

    Fill the form or just WhatsApp us. Send reports if you have them. It's fine if you don't.

  2. We call you

    Usually within 2 hours, between 9 AM and 9 PM. We'll ask a few questions and mostly listen.

  3. We ask the hospitals

    We send your reports to two or three good hospitals and get their opinion and cost back.

  4. You decide

    Hospital, room, travel and help, all in one plan. You choose what you want and skip what you don't.

  5. We stay in touch

    We check on you every day during treatment, and remind you about medicines and check-ups once you're home.

Questions

Questions people ask us

Do I have to pay Irynt anything?

No. Finding hospitals, getting estimates, booking appointments and helping with insurance papers is free for you. Some extras do cost money, like a room, a cab or a helper at the hospital. We'll always tell you the price first, and you can say no.

Then how do you make money?

Hospitals and some of our partners (rooms, cabs) pay us a fee for sending patients. We're upfront about it. The estimate you see is exactly what the hospital gave us, and we don't add anything on top.

Are you doctors? Will you tell us which treatment to take?

No, we're not doctors and we won't advise on treatment. The hospital's specialists do that. Our job is everything around it: finding the right place, arranging travel and stay, and keeping things on track.

Which hospitals do you work with?

Only hospitals accredited by NABH or JCI, in the big medical cities. For your case we'll usually get you two or three options so you can compare the doctor, the cost and how long you'll need to stay.

Is my medical information safe with you?

Yes. We only send your reports to hospitals you agree to, and only for your treatment. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Can you help with long treatments like cancer?

Yes, and honestly that's where we're most useful. Monthly rooms near the hospital, cabs for every visit, medicines, and someone to help the family member who's staying with the patient.

We live outside India. Can you still help?

Yes. We get the hospital's letter for your medical visa, pick you up from the airport, arrange a place to stay and a translator if you need one, and check on you every day.

What if it's an emergency?

Please don't wait for us. Call 112 in India (or your local emergency number) or go straight to the nearest hospital.

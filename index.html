Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Irynt's service free?

Yes, care coordination (finding hospitals, comparing estimates, booking appointments, insurance paperwork help and follow-up) is free for patients. Optional services such as a personal assistant, stay, cabs or medicines have a cost, and we always tell you the price before you confirm anything.

How does Irynt make money?

Partner hospitals and service providers may pay Irynt a facilitation fee for coordinating patients. We tell you this openly, show you the hospital's own estimate, and never add hidden charges to it. You are free to choose any option, or none.

Are you a hospital? Do you give medical advice?

No. Irynt is a care coordination service. We do not diagnose or treat. All medical opinions and treatment come from qualified doctors at the hospital you choose.

Which hospitals do you work with?

We work with hospitals accredited by recognised bodies such as NABH or JCI, in major medical cities across India. We share 2–3 options for your case so you can compare doctors, costs and recovery time.

Is my medical information safe?

Your reports are shared only with the hospitals and doctors you approve, only for your treatment. We do not sell your data.

Can you help if my treatment is long, like cancer treatment?

Yes. This is where we help most: longer stays near the hospital, monthly room options, medicine refills, cabs for each visit and support for the family member staying with the patient.

I live outside India. Can you help?

Yes. We arrange the hospital invitation letter for the medical visa, airport pickup, stay, translator and daily support during treatment.

What if it's an emergency?

Irynt is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 112 in India or your local emergency number, or go to the nearest hospital immediately.

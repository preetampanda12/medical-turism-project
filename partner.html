One point of contact

Domestic & international

Families from tier-2/3 Indian cities, and international patients needing visa support.

How a referral works

  1. Case sent: patient summary and reports, with consent.
  2. Your opinion and estimate: ideally within 24–72 hours.
  3. Patient chooses: we present 2–3 options with your original estimate.
  4. Admission: we confirm dates, travel and stay. The patient pays the hospital directly.
  5. Follow-up: we stay in touch after discharge.

What we ask of partners

  • A named point of contact (international desk, marketing or patient relations)
  • Timely opinions and written estimates
  • Fair, consistent pricing: the same or better than for walk-in patients
  • A clear, written facilitation agreement

Also looking for

Clean, affordable stays near hospitals, reliable cab operators, trained care assistants, pharmacies that deliver and translators.

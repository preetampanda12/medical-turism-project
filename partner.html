How a referral works

  1. 1. Case sentPatient summary + reports, shared with the patient's consent.
  2. 2. Your opinion & estimateIdeally within 24–72 hours, from your international / patient desk.
  3. 3. Patient choosesWe present 2–3 options side by side with your original estimate.
  4. 4. AdmissionWe confirm dates, travel and stay. The patient pays the hospital directly.
  5. 5. Follow-upWe stay in touch with the patient and your team after discharge.

What we ask of partners

  • A named point of contactInternational desk, marketing or patient relations.
  • Timely opinions & written estimatesSo families can decide quickly.
  • Fair, consistent pricingThe same or better than walk-in patients.
  • A facilitation agreementClear, written terms for referrals.
